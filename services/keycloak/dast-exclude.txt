# URLs the DAST lane keeps out of the scan (scripts/dast-lane.mjs): one regular expression per line.
# The contract has every operation of the Admin REST API; the scan covers the operations the pack's
# cases cover (registry/users.md): a realm's users, their count and one user. A scan of the whole
# contract does not end within the job's time.
# The realm list and realm creation:
^https?://[^/]+/admin/realms/?$
# The realm itself, read, updated and deleted:
^https?://[^/]+/admin/realms/[^/]+/?$
# Every other resource of a realm:
^https?://[^/]+/admin/realms/[^/]+/(?!users([/?]|$)).*$
# The user profile configuration, and what lies below one user (credentials, groups, role
# mappings, sessions, impersonation, e-mail actions):
^https?://[^/]+/admin/realms/[^/]+/users/profile([/?].*)?$
^https?://[^/]+/admin/realms/[^/]+/users/[^/?]+/.*$
